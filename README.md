### About me

I am an Electrical Engineer graduated from the Federal University of Sergipe (UFS), with a specialization in Power Systems (Eletrotécnica). Currently, I am expanding my expertise in Automotive Engineering as a scholar in the Technological Residency Program at CIn-UFPE, in partnership with Stellantis.

My professional background includes practical experience in Maintenance and Reliability Engineering, with a strong focus on High-Voltage Systems and Subtransmission. I am highly enthusiastic about Control and Automation, Industrial Instrumentation, Energy Processing, and the ongoing Energy Transition.

Driven by technological innovation, I am constantly exploring the intersection of engineering and data. My current focus includes Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Computational Modeling. I leverage tools like Python, MATLAB, and C, alongside Agile methodologies, to drive R&D initiatives and automate processes.
